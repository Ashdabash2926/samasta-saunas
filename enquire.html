— The journey

Five steps, six weeks, a sauna at yours.

Slow, careful, and honest. Each step has its own pace, with no pressure to rush from one to the next.

  1. Send an enquiry

    Drop us your details and tell us a little about your garden and what you're hoping for. We'll get back to you within a couple of days to fix a date.

  2. Consultation at the farm

    Come up to Leeds. Brew, sauna, chair, conversation. Try a model, ask anything, and we'll talk through your plot in proper detail.

  3. Deposit & build slot

    A deposit secures your sauna and your six-week build window. We start making it, panel by panel, that same week.

  4. Final payment

    Paid in full just before delivery, so we can leave the farm with a clean conscience and a full lorry.

  5. Delivery & install

    We arrive in the morning. Two days later, the sauna is built, the fire is lit, and the kettle's on at your place this time.
