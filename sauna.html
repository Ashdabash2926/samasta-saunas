    — Materials

    Three honest things, working together.

    Every panel begins as a board, every board as a tree. We pick materials that breathe, age well, and answer to the hand.

    — i.

    British red cedar

    Aromatic, rot-resistant, soft to the touch. Cut down the road and clad on the bench, it warms the room before the fire's even lit.

    — ii.

    Sheep's wool

    British wool, breathing softly between the walls. Holds heat like a Yorkshire jumper — quiet, natural, and kinder than mineral foams.

    — iii.

    Bespoke floors

    Drawn to your space. We measure twice, plane once, and finish in something safe enough to sit on barefoot the day it lands.

    — Modular

    Built here. Rebuilt at yours. Two days.

    Your sauna is made — every joint, every plank — at our farm in Leeds. When it's ready, we pack it down, drive it to you, and put it back together where you'll use it.

    1. — i.Built panel-by-panel at the farm over six weeks.
    2. — ii.Disassembled, wrapped, loaded.
    3. — iii.Delivered to a flat, prepared plot at your home.
    4. — iv.Reassembled in two days — first sweat on day three.

    — Before we arrive

    Site requirements

    Your plot must be verified as flat before the install date. We'll talk you through what that means at the consultation — most gardens are fine with a small amount of prep.

    If the site isn't ready when we turn up, the install will be put back and a return-visit fee will apply. We mention this not to be stern — only because two days off the bench is two days we owed someone else.
